It answers the questions a bill raises

A bill tells you what you owe. It doesn't tell you what happened. Meter Matters does.

Was it me, or the weather?

Every bill is compared with the same weeks a year earlier, with the weather taken out. A cold February isn't mistaken for a wasteful one.

Or was it the price?

Each change in a bill is split into three parts: what you used, what the weather did, and what the rate did. The three add up to the change exactly.

What will the next one be?

A forecast for the next statement, with a range that comes from how far past forecasts were off, not from a guess.

Did something change?

A step up in usage the weather doesn't explain gets found, sized and priced. Give it a name, like "new heat pump", and it stops being flagged.

What is this costing me?

A space heater, an EV, a dripping tap. Priced at your own rate, with the carbon from your own grid.

Am I actually doing better?

Streaks, levels and badges that count only bills that really went down after the weather is taken out. A mild winter earns nothing.

Getting bills in

Use whatever your utility already gives you. Nothing to scan, nothing to retype, unless you want to.

  • Upload a usage exportGreen Button XML or a CSV from your utility's website. Columns are matched by name, and it asks if it can't tell.
  • Upload a bill historyThe account history download most utilities offer. Each row becomes a statement.
  • Forward bill emailsYou get a private address. Set a filter once and new bills arrive on their own.
  • Connect GmailIt finds bill emails itself. Read-only, and you can disconnect at any time.
  • Type one inAmount, dates and usage, by hand. Good for a first bill or an odd one.
  • More than one homeEach home is kept separate, with its own pages and its own weather.

Your bills stay yours

Meter Matters exists to read your bills to you, not to do anything else with them.

  • Nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising. There are no trackers on the app or on this page.
  • Weather is looked up by ZIP code only. Your address is never asked for.
  • Deleting your account deletes every bill, export and setting, straight away.
  • The code is open source on GitHub, so you can see exactly what it does.
